## Scope And Audience

This specialization is the Ontario provincial module for AgentInvestigate private investigation and private security work. It is for skill authors, evaluators, and future Canadian jurisdiction modules that need source-backed issue spotting for Ontario investigator licensing, security licensing, training, professional conduct, permitted authorities, restrictions, privacy interaction, reporting, security operations, and materially relevant provincial laws.

This module is not legal advice, licensing approval, training certification, eligibility approval, privacy compliance certification, investigative authorization, security authorization, employment-action approval, use-of-force qualification, weapons qualification, or law-enforcement authority.

## Critical Boundary

Ontario sources can identify provincial issue areas, but they do not by themselves authorize private investigative or security work without current licensing, authority, role, purpose, and qualified review.

Federal, municipal, site-specific, employer, client, contract, collective agreement, and insurance requirements may still apply. Use the Canada federal specialization when federal privacy, criminal-law, evidence, human-rights, federally regulated workplace, or federal labour issues are present.
