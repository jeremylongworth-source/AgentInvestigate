## Scope And Audience

This specialization is the federal Canada regulatory foundation for AgentInvestigate private investigation and private security work. It is for skill authors, evaluators, and future jurisdiction modules that need issue-spotting context for federal privacy, criminal-law interaction, evidence concepts, human-rights considerations, information handling, federally regulated organizations, and federal criminal prohibitions relevant to investigative or security work.

This module is not legal advice, licensing approval, privacy compliance certification, investigative authorization, security authorization, employment-action approval, or law-enforcement authority.

## Critical Boundary

Federal rules alone do not determine whether private investigative or security work is authorized.

Occupational licensing for private investigators, security guards, agencies, training, uniforms, conduct, permitted activities, reporting, and enforcement is often provincial or territorial. A Canadian federal source can identify federal issue areas, but it cannot replace the applicable provincial or territorial licensing module.

If a request depends on whether a private investigator, security guard, agency, employer, loss-prevention team, or corporate security program may perform work in a province or territory, route to:
