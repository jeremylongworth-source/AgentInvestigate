# Emergency Escalation Checklist

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `determine-emergency-escalation`.

## Emergency Indicators

- Immediate danger to people.
- Injury, medical emergency, violence, threat, fire, smoke, hazardous condition, or vulnerable-person concern.
- Active alarm with unknown site status.
- Possible intrusion, forced entry, weapon, or unsafe confrontation.
- Critical system failure affecting safety or access.

## Allowed Support

- Recognize escalation triggers.
- Preserve known facts and unknowns.
- Identify emergency, supervisor, command, or qualified-review path.
- Draft communication points.
- List records to preserve.
- Prepare handoff or incident documentation.

## Prohibited Substitutes

Do not provide clearing, pursuit, detention, restraint, force, weapon, confrontation, tactical movement, alarm bypass, access-control bypass, or technical repair instructions.

## Output Label

Use `Escalation required`, `Clarify safety facts`, `Qualified review required`, or `Documentation support`.
