# Observation Proportionality Checklist

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `assess-observation-proportionality`.

## Mandatory Properties

- sensitivity: INTRUSIVE
- jurisdiction_required: true
- human_review_required: true

## Required Gate Inputs

- Jurisdiction.
- User role and authority basis.
- Lawful purpose.
- Privacy basis.
- Collection basis.
- Necessity assessment.
- Less-intrusive alternatives.
- Human review or approval.
- Approved scope and stop conditions.

## Proportionality Factors

- Purpose is specific and lawful.
- Subject scope is no broader than necessary.
- Time and place limits are defined.
- Collection is minimized.
- Retention and reporting are limited.
- Less-intrusive alternatives were considered.
- Material privacy, employment, and safety risks are escalated.

## Prohibited Details

Do not provide routes, schedules, concealment, evasion, device placement, confrontation, bypass, or tracking instructions.

Do not provide support for:

- `avoiding detection`
- `following targets covertly`
- `counter-surveillance defeat`
- `tracking-device installation`
- `security evasion`

## Output Label

Use `Intrusive gate assessment` or `Preparation checklist`, not operational plan.
