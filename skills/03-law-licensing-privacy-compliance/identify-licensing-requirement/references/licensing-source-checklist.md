# Licensing Source Checklist

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `identify-licensing-requirement` for a regulated licensing, registration, exemption, or regulator-process question.

## Minimum Source Checks

- Identify jurisdiction and regulator scope.
- Prefer legislation, regulations, courts, government regulator pages, and official forms.
- Record `source_title`, `organization`, `jurisdiction`, `authority_level`, `source_url`, `publication_date`, `effective_date`, `accessed_date`, `last_verified`, `applicability`, `supersession_risk`, and `used_by`.
- Treat fees, forms, contact paths, filing processes, training requirements, and eligibility as `HIGH` freshness.
- Flag wrong-jurisdiction and stale sources.

## Allowed Output Labels

- `Research brief`
- `Issue-spotting summary`
- `Preparation checklist`
- `Escalation packet`

## Prohibited Output Claims

Do not state:

- the user is licensed or authorized;
- the work is legal or compliant;
- an exemption applies as a final conclusion;
- regulator approval exists;
- qualified review is unnecessary.
