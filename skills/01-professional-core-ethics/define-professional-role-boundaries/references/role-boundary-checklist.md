# Role Boundary Checklist

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `define-professional-role-boundaries`.

## Checks

- Identify the requested work.
- Identify the user's stated role.
- Identify client or organizational context.
- Separate private investigation, private security, shared support, and qualified-review responsibilities.
- State missing authority, jurisdiction, licence, certification, or policy facts.
- Block role expansion into prohibited conduct.

## Boundary Statement

Include what the agent can support, what a responsible human must decide, and what is outside scope.
