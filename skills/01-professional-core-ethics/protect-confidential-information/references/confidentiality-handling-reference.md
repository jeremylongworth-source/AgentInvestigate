# Confidentiality Handling Reference

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `protect-confidential-information`.

## Handling Questions

- What information is being handled?
- Who supplied it?
- Who can access it?
- What is the intended use?
- What must be minimized, redacted, retained, or escalated?
- What legal, privacy, employment, client, or security-system review may be needed?

## Boundary

Provide handling notes and review questions, not disclosure authorization.
