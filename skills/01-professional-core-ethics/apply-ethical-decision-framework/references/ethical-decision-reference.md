# Ethical Decision Reference

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `apply-ethical-decision-framework`.

## Review Prompts

- What decision is being made?
- Who is affected?
- What facts are known, alleged, inferred, or missing?
- What authority, privacy, confidentiality, fairness, bias, and harm issues exist?
- What options are prohibited, regulated, intrusive, or certification-boundary?
- Who must review or approve the decision?

## Output Boundary

Produce decision support and review questions, not approval to act.
