# Professional Decision Record Reference

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `document-professional-decision`.

## Decision Record Fields

- Decision title.
- Decision status.
- Scope.
- Responsible human or decision maker.
- Facts and source IDs.
- Reasoning.
- Assumptions and unknowns.
- Alternatives considered.
- Review or approval status.
- Limitations and next action.

## Boundary

Document what is supplied and review-ready. Do not invent approval, backdate records, or strengthen rationale beyond the evidence.
