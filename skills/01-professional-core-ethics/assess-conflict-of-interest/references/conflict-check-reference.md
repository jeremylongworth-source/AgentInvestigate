# Conflict Check Reference

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `assess-conflict-of-interest`.

## Conflict Indicators

- Prior involvement with a party or subject.
- Personal, financial, employment, family, or reporting relationship.
- Competing duties to client, employer, subject, regulator, or public safety.
- Confidential information from another matter.
- Bias, retaliation, or appearance-of-impropriety concern.

## Output Boundary

Flag conflict issues and review questions. Do not decide waiver, disqualification, legal privilege, or discipline.
