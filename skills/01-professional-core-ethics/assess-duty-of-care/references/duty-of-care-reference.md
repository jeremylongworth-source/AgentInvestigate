# Duty Of Care Reference

## When To Read

Read this reference before using `assess-duty-of-care`.

## Care Considerations

- Role and authority.
- Affected people and foreseeable harm.
- Safety, privacy, confidentiality, and fairness.
- Documentation and preservation needs.
- Escalation and qualified-review triggers.

## Boundary

Identify issues and review paths. Do not decide legal duty, breach, negligence, liability, or compliance.
